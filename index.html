教会简介

素里中国福音教会（Surrey Christian Evangelical Church）由原属中国福音教会而居住在素里地区的几个家庭，在三一真神的呼召和感动下，经过长时间祷告后于 2008 年 2 月所创建。

2015 年教会完成正式注册，现在是在美南浸信会下，独立注册、自我管理的地方教会。教会固定在每周日下午分别有普通话敬拜和英文敬拜，以及成人及儿童主日学。

About Us

Surrey Christian Evangelical Church (SCEC) was founded in February 2008 by several families living in the Surrey area under the calling of the Triune God. The church completed its official registration in 2015.

We are an independently registered, self-governing local church under the Southern Baptist Convention. We provide Mandarin worship, English worship, adult and children Sunday school every week.
