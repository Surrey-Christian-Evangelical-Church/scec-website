# 青少年团契(Youth Group)

**Source URL:** https://scecchinese.com/youth-group/

---

欢迎加入素里中国福音教会青少年团契。我们的青少年团契是由一群充满活力、对基督信仰有热烈追求的年轻人组成。他们每周五晚7：00 &#8211; 9：30都有团契活动，每周日下午2：15-3：45有专门的英文崇拜。若想了解他们更多的信息，请加入他们的[instagram](https://www.instagram.com/scecyouth/?utm_source=ig_embed)账户，或加入他们的[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/819364824802794)群组。

Welcome to join SCEC youth group. Our youth group consists of young people who are dedicated to learning about Jesus Christ and growing in the faith.  They have activities at very Friday night from 7:00 pm to 9:30 pm. Their weekly worships are held in every Sunday afternoon from 2:15 pm to 3:45 pm. To know more about our youth group, please follow their [instagram](https://www.instagram.com/scecyouth/?utm_source=ig_embed) or join their [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/819364824802794) group.
